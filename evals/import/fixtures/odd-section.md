# Four-Week Spanish Restart

A short plan to get conversational Spanish back after a long break. Forty minutes a day, five days a week.

## Week 1 · Sounds and survival phrases

- Review the alphabet, stress rules and the 100 most common words.
- Practice greetings, ordering food and asking directions out loud, 15 minutes a day.
- Exit: introduce yourself and order a meal without notes.

## Week 2 · Present tense

- Regular -ar, -er and -ir verbs, then ser, estar, tener and ir.
- Write five sentences a day about your routine and read them aloud.
- Exit: describe your day in the present tense for one minute.

## Week 3 · Past tense

- Preterite of regular verbs and the common irregulars.
- Listen to one short podcast episode a day and note ten new words.
- Exit: tell a story about last weekend.

## Week 4 · Conversation

- Two 30-minute conversation sessions with a tutor.
- Rewatch a favorite series in Spanish with subtitles off.
- Exit: hold a ten-minute conversation about your week.

## Ongoing

- Review flashcards whenever you have a spare minute, in any week.
- Keep a vocabulary notebook and add to it as you read.
