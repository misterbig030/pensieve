# Plan Page Tabs Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the track page's About tab with a small summary-and-split band on the Plan tab. Give Materials and Activity a right-hand column; Activity's is a calendar of real study dates.

**Architecture:** Three pure helpers carry the logic:
- the split bar's parts, extracted from `TimeSplitCard` so the card and the band share them;
- the study calendar's weeks;
- a week's check-in total.

New tab components (`PlanGlance`, `MaterialsTab`, the rewritten `ActivityTab`) lay them out. `MaterialsList` gains a `bare` mode for the tab. `page.tsx` drops the About tab and passes each tab what it needs.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 App Router (server page, client components where state is needed), React 19, Tailwind 4, Vitest with `renderToStaticMarkup` (node env, effects don't run).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-plan-tabs-design.md` (mock: `docs/mockups/plan-tabs.html`, options P1, M1, and A1 with A2's grid)

## Global Constraints

- Days are UTC days and weeks run Monday to Sunday, as in `lib/activity.ts`.
- **Narrow layout:** below 900 px (`max-[900px]:`), every two-column layout becomes one column.
  - The band's sides stack, summary first.
  - The Materials panels go above the list, and "Jump to" is hidden.
  - The Activity grid panel goes above the list.
- **Right columns:** 320 px wide and `sticky top-0`. Left columns are at most 820 px.
- **Copy, verbatim:**
  - Band: "The plan", "More" / "Less", "How the N h split", "The plan's call" / "Your call", "Why".
  - Materials panels: "N materials", "Checked", "Jump to".
  - Activity panel: "Your study days".
  - Under the grid: "Estimated finish: 26 Dec"-style date.
  - Week headings: "2 days · 2.5 h", "3 sessions · 4 h" or "1 day · 2 sessions · 4 h". Zero parts are left out.
- **Local DB is production.** Live checks only read; never click planning, Write, Mark complete or Log.

## Review Focus

1. **A plan with no split** (saved before splits existed): the band shows the summary full width, with no bar and no "Why". Test: Task 2.
2. **An old split with no `formats`:** the bar shows reading and one practice part, labelled with `split.practice`. Test: Task 1.
3. **Track age:**
   - A track created more than 12 weeks ago shows only the last 12 weeks.
   - A track created mid-week shows its earlier days that week as blank, not empty.
   - Test: Task 3.
4. **Two check-ins on one date** fill one cell that names both. Test: Task 3.
5. **More than 12 materials:** the Materials tab shows them all, with no "Show all", and every "Jump to" anchor exists in the markup. Test: Task 4.
