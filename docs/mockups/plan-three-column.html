Plan page in three columns: outline, pane, materials

The Materials tab goes away, and the materials sit in a column on the right of the Plan tab. Materials the selected week uses are lit up, so the pane and the list point at the same things. Two ways to place it.

T1 · Materials column beside the outline and pane, under the bandThe summary band spans the whole width. Under it: the outline, the pane, then the materials column. The column stays in place as the outline scrolls. The selected week's materials are highlighted and come first.
